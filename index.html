What it does today

A paper canvas with your pattern, a 3D viewer with the avatar, and the usual tools in the usual order. The interface speaks Spanish for now.

Drafting

Pen, rectangle, notches and seam allowances, with the grainline drawn and the dimensions written where they measure. Darts come in with your DXF.

Sewing

Sew pieces automatically or edge to edge, and watch the seams close in 3D.

Drape

Fabric simulated in real time on the avatar, with WebGPU inside the browser. A fit map from the first frame.

Reads
DXF-AAMA · DXF-ASTM · Gerber and Lectra dialects
Writes
DXF-AAMA · PDF at 1:1 · BOM (CSV) · glTF · USDZ · 360° turn
Copilot
Ask it to add a dart or to audit the seam allowances. It uses the same tools you do, and every change can be undone. With your own key: Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek or Ollama.

How it's made

In the open, one decision at a time

Each change starts as a written design, gets a review, and lands with its tests. The specs, the research and the mistakes are in the repository, so the history tells itself.
