# OpenClo Strategy: The Open Standard Manifesto (100% Free)

## 1. The Vision: The "Linux of Fashion"
By making OpenClo 100% free (no SaaS tiers, no marketplace tax), we remove every single barrier to entry. Our goal is not to sell licenses, but to **standardize the garment design process globally** via the `.openclo` semantic format.

> **Amendment, 2026-10-05 (Samuel).** The core stays free and open source. Vina's own hosted AI agent is a paid tier, and it is how the project bills: "no SaaS tiers" no longer holds. See `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-ia-una-superficie-design.md`, decisions D2 and D3.

## 2. Why "Free" is the Most Aggressive Business Move
Legacy competitors (CLO3D, Optitex) survive on high-priced gatekeeping. 
- **The Performance Moat**: OpenClo is now powered by **WebGPU and TSL (Three.js Shading Language)**. We offer 10–50x more performance than any other browser-based tool. By being free and technically superior, we make proprietary silos obsolete.
- **Viral Adoption**: Students, independent designers, and factories will adopt OpenClo instantly because it's professional-grade, zero-installation, and cost-free.
- **Industrial Precision**: With **Gauss-Legendre** math (<0.1mm error) and 3,100+ automated tests, we provide the reliability that factories previously only found in $10k+ software.

## 3. The Role of the Marketplace (Pure Collaboration)
The Marketplace becomes a **Public Commons of Knowledge**. 
- Designers share `.oskill` functional plugins to solve collective problems (e.g., sustainability calculators, new grading methods).
- **Network Effect**: Every new skill added makes OpenClo more valuable than CLO3D’s closed ecosystem.

## 4. Sustainability & ROI (The Enterprise Bridge)
Sustainability in a 100% free model comes from **Infrastructure and Influence**.

### A. Strategic Consulting & Customization (Enterprise)
Big brands (e.g., Adidas, Nike) need OpenClo integrated into their internal ERPs, PLMs, and factory systems. They don't pay for the software; they pay for **Deployment, Training, and Custom Feature Development** (e.g., specific fit-analysis algorithms for high-performance gear).

### B. The OpenClo Foundation (Blender Model)
Following the **Blender/Linux model**, the project is sustained by corporate sponsorships. Brands contribute because they want to ensure the open infrastructure they rely on stays healthy and evolves.

### C. Digital Product Creation (DPC) Efficiency
OpenClo drastically reduces the **Time-to-Market**. By providing industrial-grade simulation for free, we help brands move to a "Zero-Sample" workflow, saving millions in physical prototyping and shipping.

## 5. Next Steps for Governance
1. **LICENSE**: Finalize the MIT/GPL strategy to protect the project's openness.
2. **The "Open Standard" Spec**: Cement the `.openclo` format as the industry standard for semantic garment data.
3. **Enterprise Tier 0**: Establish the first "Industrial Partners" (Beta testers in manufacturing).

## 6. Conclusion
Being 100% free is not "losing money"; it's **capturing the entire market's attention and trust**. OpenClo will win by being the only tool that is technically superior, mathematically precise, and universally accessible.
