# Brand Strategy: OpenClo.ai (AI-First Narrative)

## 1. The Power of .ai
Choosing **openclo.ai** is a declaration of intent. It shifts the perception of the tool from a "Geometric CAD" to a "Cognitive Design Engine." 

## 2. The Core Narrative: "The Intelligence within the Fabric"
The focus is no longer on the lines you draw, but on the agent that helps you draw them. 
- **Tagline**: The AI Engine for Professional Fashion Engineering.
- **Narrative**: "Design with an agent that understands anatomy, physics, and construction as deeply as you do."

## 3. Centering AI in the Landing Page
The landing page must prioritize the **situated agency** of OpenClo:
1. **Hero**: Visual focus on the Copilot's "Brain" or "Vision" interface.
2. **Key Feature**: "Semantic Pattern Recognition" (The AI knows it's a sleeve).
3. **Key Feature**: "Durable Design Memory" (The AI remembers your style).
4. **Trust Factor**: "Deterministic Physics" (The AI validates with Rust-speed precision).

## 4. Subdomain Strategy
- **openclo.ai**: The high-impact landing page showcasing the AI's brilliance.
- **app.openclo.ai**: The workspace where humans and AI design together.

## 5. Marketing Hook
"Stop drawing vectors. Start collaborating with an expert fashion agent. 100% Free. 100% Open Source."

## 6. Technical Implication
The `openclo.ai` root will live in a separate, SEO-optimized repository using **Astro or Next.js** to ensure the fastest possible "Time to Interactive" for new visitors.
