# Brand Strategy: OpenClo.ai (The Cognitive Design Engine)

## 1. The Power of .ai
Choosing **openclo.ai** is a declaration of intent. It shifts the perception of the tool from a "Geometric CAD" to a **"Cognitive Design Engine."** We aren't just digitizing lines; we are digitizing fashion intelligence.

## 2. The Core Narrative: "The Expert Agent in the Room"
The focus is no longer on the tools you use, but on the **situated agent** that helps you design.
- **Tagline**: The Cognitive Design Engine for Professional Fashion Engineering.
- **Narrative**: "Design with an agent that understands anatomy, physics, and construction as deeply as a master tailor."

## 3. Centering the Cognitive Value Proposition
The landing page (`openclo.ai`) must prioritize the **situated agency** and **semantic depth** of the engine:
1.  **Hero**: Visual focus on the Copilot's "Brain" and the **Semantic Graph** visualization.
2.  **Semantic Recognition**: "The AI knows it's a sleeve." Explain how the engine understands anatomical relationships.
3.  **Durable Design Memory**: "Your style, remembered." Showcase how the AI learns fit standards and aesthetic preferences across sessions.
4.  **Deterministic Physics**: "Validate with XPBD Precision." Highlight the Rust-based engine for high-speed, accurate 3D draping.
5.  **Silent Ingestion**: "Zero-Friction Migration." Mention compatibility with legacy formats (Seamly2D, DXF) as an effortless, intelligent hydration service.

## 4. Subdomain Strategy
- **openclo.ai**: The high-impact landing page showcasing the engine's intelligence and the **"Open Source AI"** mission.
- **app.openclo.ai**: The high-performance workspace where humans and AI synthesize garments together.

## 5. Marketing Hook
"Stop drawing vectors. Start collaborating with an expert fashion agent. 100% Free. 100% Open Source. 100% Professional."

## 6. Technical Implication
The `openclo.ai` root will live in a separate, SEO-optimized repository using **Astro or Next.js** to ensure the fastest possible "Time to Interactive" for new visitors. The app will maintain its **React/Rust/WASM** stack for maximum browser performance.
