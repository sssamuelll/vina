# Design Spec: OpenClo Landing Page (Strategic Update)

## 1. Core Narrative: "The Universal Fashion IDE"
OpenClo is the professional bridge between traditional pattern making and the future of AI-driven simulation. Our primary hook is **Unrestricted Interoperability**: the ability to design, simulate, and export using the same industrial standards (DXF-AAMA) used by major global brands.

## 2. Updated Page Structure

### Hero: The Industrial Powerhouse
- **Headline**: "Fashion Engineering Without Boundaries."
- **Sub-headline**: "The first open-source 2D/3D IDE for professional pattern makers. Native DXF-AAMA support, Wasm-powered XPBD physics, and a situated AI Copilot."
- **Primary CTA**: "Launch IDE" (Direct link).
- **Secondary CTA**: "View on GitHub."

### Section 1: Professional Interoperability (The New Focus)
- **Copy**: "Speak the Industry Standard."
- **Key Points**: 
  - Direct import/export of **DXF-AAMA** files (Gerber, Lectra, Optitex compatible).
  - Production-ready Tech Pack generation.
  - Seamless migration from legacy open-source tools (Seamly2D/Valentina).

### Section 2: The Situated Copilot (The Brain)
- **Copy**: "An AI Assistant with Spatial Awareness."
- **Focus**: Show how the AI recognizes armholes, necklines, and stress maps autonomously.

### Section 3: High-Fidelity Physics (The Engine)
- **Copy**: "Real-time Draping at 60 FPS."
- **Focus**: Multi-piece simulation, physical seam constraints, and MacBook M2/M3 optimization.

## 3. Visual Language
- **IDE Aesthetic**: Maintain the VS Code/Dark mode look.
- **Industrial Accents**: Use technical drawings, blueprint overlays, and DXF point-clouds as background textures.
- **Performance Badge**: "Built with Rust & WebAssembly" should be a visible badge of quality.

## 4. SEO & Outreach Strategy
- Target Keywords: "Open Source Fashion CAD", "DXF-AAMA Web Editor", "AI Pattern Making", "Professional Cloth Simulation".
- Conversion: Focus on professional designers looking for a lightweight alternative to CLO3D.
